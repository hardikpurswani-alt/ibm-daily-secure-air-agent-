# India CPCB National Air Quality Index (NAQI) & GRAP Policy Framework
Source: Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) & Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM)
URL: https://airquality.cpcb.gov.in/
Published: 2024

## Indian NAQI Categories
- **Good (0–50):** Minimal health impact.
- **Satisfactory (51–100):** Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
- **Moderately Polluted (101–200):** Breathing discomfort to people with lung disease, asthma, and heart disease.
- **Poor (201–300):** Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure.
- **Very Poor (301–400):** Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure; pronounced effect on people with lung and heart disease.
- **Severe (401–500):** Respiratory effects even on healthy people and serious impacts on people with lung/heart disease.

## Monitored Pollutants in CPCB CAAQMS
CPCB continuous ambient stations monitor **PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃, and NH₃ (Ammonia)**. Lead (**Pb**) is monitored via periodic gravimetric filter sampling rather than continuous hourly telemetry on CAAQMS feeds.

## Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) Civic & Policy Measures
- **Stage I (Poor, NAQI 201–300):** Strict enforcement of dust mitigation at construction/demolition sites, mechanized road sweeping and water sprinkling, zero tolerance for open municipal waste/biomass burning, and Pollution Under Control (PUC) vehicle enforcement.
- **Stage II (Very Poor, NAQI 301–400):** Restrictions on diesel generator sets (except essential services), enhanced parking fees to discourage private vehicles, increased metro/electric bus frequency, and ban on coal/firewood use in commercial tandoors/eateries.
- **Stage III (Severe, NAQI 401–450):** Suspension of non-essential construction and demolition activities, restrictions on older BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel four-wheelers, and recommendations for primary school remote/hybrid learning.
- **Stage IV (Severe+, NAQI > 450):** Entry ban on non-essential diesel trucks, temporary halt on highway/linear infrastructure dust-generating works, and public/private office work-from-home advisories (50% capacity).
