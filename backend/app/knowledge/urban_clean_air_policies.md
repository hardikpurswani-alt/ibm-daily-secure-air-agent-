# Community Actions & Clean Air Policy Measures
Source: UNEP Clean Air Actions & Municipal Air Quality Playbooks
URL: https://www.unep.org/explore-topics/air
Published: 2024

## High-Impact Community & Individual Mitigation Actions
1. **Shared Mobility & Transit Shift:** Replacing solo internal-combustion commutes with rail, electric buses, cycling (during low-AQI windows), or 3-person carpooling reduces tailpipe NO₂, CO, and secondary PM2.5 precursor emissions.
2. **Zero Open Burning & Organic Composting:** Eliminating garden leaf, crop residue, and municipal trash burning prevents acute local PM2.5 and toxic dioxin/PAH plumes; community composting converts biomass into soil mulch safely.
3. **Construction & Road Dust Suppression:** Enforcing tarpaulin truck covers, perimeter dust screens, wet suppression, and unpaved shoulder paving sharply cuts urban PM10 spikes.
4. **Urban Canopy & Green Buffers:** Planting native, broad-leaf, drought-resilient tree belts along high-traffic corridors intercepts coarse dust and cools urban heat islands (reducing photochemical O₃ formation). Note: vegetation complements rather than replaces source emission cuts.
5. **Community Clean Air Hubs:** Opening public libraries, schools, and community halls with HEPA/MERV-13 filtration as accessible clean-air shelters protects elderly residents and low-income households during severe pollution episodes.
6. **Citizen Reporting & Policy Advocacy:** Reporting illegal industrial smoke, idling commercial fleets, and waste burning through municipal 311 / Sameer CPCB portals accelerates enforcement.
